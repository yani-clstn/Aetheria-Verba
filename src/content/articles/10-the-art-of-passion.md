"If love wasn't for me, remove my desire to love and to be loved" used to be my favorite prayer. 

I used to think love had to look like one person, one home, one forever—having someone by your side.

But love.. you can find it anywhere even without searching for it.

Love is when your eyes sparkle looking at a beautiful view that you saw for the first time. Love is when your heart gets excited trying a new hobby. Love is when you do that little dance while eating delicious food. Love is curiosity, love is learning a new language. Love is doing things you didn't know you're capable of doing. Love is yearning for doing everything scared, even without mastering them. 

Having a lot of passions and yearning to try different things is love.

Not the romantic kind, but the bigger kind. The kind that refuses your heart to stay small, but to explore how big the world is—to maximize your capacity of loving. 

The kind that says "If love can't find me, let me find a million things to love instead."

So maybe, I wasn't incapable of love, but love is me. Maybe I was loving the world instead.

And that will always be more than enough.
