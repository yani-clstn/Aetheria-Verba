Yearning and longing may be related, but they carry different depths of emotion. 

Longing is the feeling of deeply wanting or missing something or someone that is absent. 

Yearning, on the other hand, goes beyond that. It is stronger, and more persistent desire that can stay with you for a long time. 

The yearning of the heart is the reaching of something or someone it cannot easily let go of. It doesn't just missed someone or something, it hopes, it wants, it desires, it moves.

*But, when it comes to that one person...*

Longing lives in the past.
Yearning dares to stay in the present. 

And maybe, that's *love.*
To mourn what was,
and still hope for what might be.
